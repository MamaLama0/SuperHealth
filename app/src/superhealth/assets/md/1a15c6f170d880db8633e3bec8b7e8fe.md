## Setting up
1. Adjust the height of the seat so that your upper arms can be parallel with the floor or a little lower while grasping the handles.
    1. After initially adjusting the seat height, spread your arms outward so that your hands are level with the handles.
    2. You may have to re-adjust the seat if your upper arms are not parallel with the floor or a little lower.
    3. Some people will find their shoulders feel more comfortable when the seat is set to a height that allows their hands to be a bit lower than their shoulders, compared to when their hands are completely level with their shoulders.
2. Adjust the pins above the handles to a position that allows you to grasp the handles with your hands as close as they can be to each other while your chest is facing the machine’s pad.
3. After sitting down with your chest against the pad, grasp the vertical handles with a neutral grip such that your palms face toward each other and slightly bend your elbows.
    1. Maintain the slight bend in your elbows throughout the set.
## Exercise technique
1. Spread the handles apart and backward until your arms and torso form a “T-shape.”
2. Reverse the motion by bringing the handles forward and toward each other until they have returned to their starting position.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop your [rear delt](https://www.notion.so/Deltoid-Posterior-Head-1905c6f170d880a29e5ec26f4d257c01?pvs=21), which is assisted by your [traps](https://www.notion.so/Trapezius-1b95c6f170d880bebf89c759b592e81f?pvs=21) and [rhomboids](https://www.notion.so/Rhomboids-1b95c6f170d880c8a84de94f5232a509?pvs=21), along with your rotator cuff’s [infraspinatus](https://www.notion.so/Infraspinatus-1905c6f170d88026aebcf3953e2e440f?pvs=21), [teres minor](https://www.notion.so/Teres-Minor-1905c6f170d88098824dc8136557c410?pvs=21), and [supraspinatus](https://www.notion.so/Supraspinatus-1905c6f170d880efb440c1614caf1c97?pvs=21).
