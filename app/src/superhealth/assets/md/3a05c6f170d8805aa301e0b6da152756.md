## Setting up
1. Add weight plates of your desired load to an axle (AKA thick bar) situated on the ground.
    1. If you lack an axle bar, you can replicate the grip demands of an axle deadlift by connecting fat grip attachments to a standard barbell shoulder-width apart.
2. While facing the middle of the axle, sit your hips backward while bending your knees until you can reach the axle with straight elbows.
3. Firmly grasp the bar with approximately a shoulder-width grip as your palms face forward (i.e., underhand grip).
4. Stand upright with the axle while keeping your elbows straight.
## Exercise technique
1. While squeezing the axle, curl it upward and back toward you in an arcing motion by bending your elbows until they are fully bent.
    1. Keep your upper arms in the same position as you avoid swinging your elbows forward through shoulder motion.
    2. While your forearms move through elbow motion, the rest of your body should remain stationary throughout your set.
2. Reverse the movement by lowering the axle in a controlled manner until you have fully straightened your elbows.
    1. Nothing else should move except for your forearms lowering in an arcing manner through elbow motion.
    2. While you do not have to perform an extremely slow lowering phase, you should avoid letting weight abruptly swing your forearms down.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set and are ready to set the axle down.
## Description
While dynamically training your [biceps](https://www.notion.so/1b95c6f170d88027b895ed4c0bb40b68?pvs=21), [brachialis](https://www.notion.so/18f5c6f170d880c181eed18b5b379fe9?pvs=21), and [brachioradialis](https://www.notion.so/1905c6f170d8808f8207e301c4acd3b3?pvs=21), this exercise can statically train your [forearm’s extrinsic finger flexors](https://www.notion.so/1bb5c6f170d88050a550fbeff6041573?pvs=21) and your [hand’s intrinsic finger flexors](https://www.notion.so/Intrinsic-Finger-Flexors-1bb5c6f170d880648d61d9b5db235bf6?pvs=21).
