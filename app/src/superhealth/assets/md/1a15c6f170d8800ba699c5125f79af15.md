## Setting up
1. Adjust the seat height to a comfortable position that will allow the lower aspect of your triceps to rest against the machine’s upper arm pads.
2. Select your desired weight with the machine’s loading pin, and sit down on its seat with an upright posture, your feet flat on the floor, and your back against the backrest.
3. After positioning your triceps against the machine’s upper arm pads, grasp the handles with an underhand grip that results in your palms facing forward.
## Exercise technique
1. Starting from a position where your elbows are straight, curl the handles upward by bending at your elbows while keeping your upper arms pinned against the machine’s arm pads (rather than allowing your upper arms to swing forward).
2. After your elbows have fully bent, reverse the motion by lowering the handles in a controlled manner until your elbows have straightened.
    1. Continue to keep your upper arms in contact with the arm pads.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
Like other curl variations, this exercise can effectively train your [biceps](https://www.notion.so/Biceps-Brachii-1b95c6f170d88027b895ed4c0bb40b68?pvs=21), [brachialis](https://www.notion.so/Brachialis-18f5c6f170d880c181eed18b5b379fe9?pvs=21), and [brachioradialis](https://www.notion.so/Brachioradialis-1905c6f170d8808f8207e301c4acd3b3?pvs=21).
